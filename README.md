## 📌 Project Status

🚧 **Ongoing Project**

This is my final year project contains the design, fabrication, experimental characterization, and finite element analysis of **3D-printed PLA/PCL/HA composite scaffolds** for potential bone tissue engineering applications.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Bone tissue engineering aims to develop biomaterial scaffolds that can provide temporary mechanical support while promoting tissue regeneration. An ideal scaffold should possess suitable mechanical strength, interconnected porosity, and structural stability.

This project focuses on the development of a composite scaffold using **Polylactic Acid (PLA)**, **Polycaprolactone (PCL)**, and **Hydroxyapatite (HA)**. The scaffold is designed using 3D CAD modelling and fabricated using **3D printing technology**.

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) is performed to evaluate the structural and mechanical behaviour of the scaffold under loading conditions. Experimental characterization of the fabricated scaffold will also be conducted, and the experimental results will be compared with the simulation results.

---

The main objectives of this project are:

1. **Fabrication of PLA/PCL/HA Composite**

   * Develop and fabricate a PLA/PCL/HA composite scaffold using 3D printing technology.

2. **Finite Element Analysis**

3. **Characterization of Fabricated Scaffold**

   * Characterize the fabricated scaffold and evaluate its relevant physical and mechanical properties.

4. **Comparison of Experimental and Simulation Results**

   * Compare the experimental results with the FEA simulation results to evaluate the accuracy and reliability of the computational model.

## 🧪 Materials Used

The scaffold composite is based on the following materials:

### PLA – Polylactic Acid

PLA is a biodegradable and biocompatible polymer commonly used in biomedical applications and 3D printing.

### PCL – Polycaprolactone

PCL is a biodegradable polymer known for its flexibility, ductility, and relatively slow degradation rate.

### HA – Hydroxyapatite

Hydroxyapatite is a bioactive ceramic material with chemical similarity to the mineral component of natural bone. It is incorporated to improve the biological and mechanical properties of the scaffold.

## 🖥️ Scaffold Design

The scaffold is designed using 3D CAD modelling software.

The design process includes:

* Development of scaffold geometry
* Selection of suitable dimensions
* Design of porous structures
* Consideration of pore interconnectivity
* Preparation of the model for 3D printing
* Export of the model in a suitable format for fabrication and simulation

The scaffold geometry is further analysed to understand its mechanical response under loading conditions.

---

## 🔬 Finite Element Analysis

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) is performed on the designed scaffold to evaluate its mechanical behaviour.

The analysis may include:

* Material property definition
* Assignment of PLA/PCL/HA composite properties
* Mesh generation
* Boundary condition application
* Load application
* Structural analysis
* Deformation analysis
* Stress distribution analysis
* Strain analysis

### Key Parameters Evaluated

* Total deformation
* Equivalent (von Mises) stress
* Equivalent strain
* Structural behaviour under compression
